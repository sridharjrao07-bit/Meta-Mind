# Decision record template

## Decision

- ID:
- Question:
- Status: `DRAFT | RATIFIED | LEAST-BAD-OPTION`
- Owner:
- Date:

## Pre-committed rule

State the rule before reviewing results. Do not rewrite it after seeing output.

## Method

Describe inputs, candidate systems, sample size, and exactly how the result was
computed.

## Raw evidence

Link committed artifacts or attach literal terminal output and payloads. A
narrative summary alone is not evidence.

## Result

Record the measured result against the rule, including failures and uncertainty.

## Decision and mitigations

State what is chosen, what is rejected, and the ranked mitigations.

## Economics and follow-ups

Record latency, token usage, estimated cost, and any later gate that must revisit
this decision.
